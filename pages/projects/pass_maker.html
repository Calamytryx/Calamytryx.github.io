Generate passwords and passphrases with browser cryptography, or estimate a pasted password locally. Nothing is saved or sent.

password generator

passphrase generator

Uses unbiased crypto.getRandomValues sampling. This is Diceware-style, not the standardized 7,776-word Diceware list: the bundled word pool has 64 words (6 bits per word).

strength estimate

Type a password to estimate its strength.

The offline estimator recognizes several common words, repeated characters, keyboard sequences, and predictable season/year patterns such as Winter2026!. Estimates are heuristic—not a proof that a password is safe or unique. Never reuse passwords across accounts.
