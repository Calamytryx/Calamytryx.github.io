TOTP authenticator

Generates standard 6-digit, 30-second TOTP codes using HMAC-SHA-1. Scan the otpauth QR with an authenticator app. Keep the secret private; it is held in this page only.

barcode generator

Generate offline SVG barcodes. Code 39 accepts uppercase letters, digits, spaces, and - . $ / + %. EAN-13 and UPC-A validate or calculate their check digits.

Code 39 data is converted to uppercase. Maximum 40 characters.
